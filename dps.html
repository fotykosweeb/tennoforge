DPS Lab

TennoForge does not invent a fake in-game DPS number. Instead, this lab combines live item stats where available with accessibility and market signals. Detailed community builds are not treated as live data.

Scoring model

Damage potential is the primary priority. Accessibility (MR/farm effort) and practical survivability/utility are secondary. Any displayed weapon stats come from public game-data sources, not fabricated benchmark results.

Build rule: pick a weapon with a strong damage profile, then preserve at least one reliable defensive layer on the Warframe.
